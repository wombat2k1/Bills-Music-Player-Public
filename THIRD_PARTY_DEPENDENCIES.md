this source repository and remains subject to its own licence terms.

## BASS

Bills Music Player can use the BASS audio library from Un4seen Developments.

BASS is not part of Bills Music Player and is not covered by the MIT licence
for this repository. Users should obtain BASS directly from its publisher and
comply with its licence.

## FFmpeg

Bills Music Player can use FFmpeg for multimedia functionality.

FFmpeg is not part of Bills Music Player and is not covered by the MIT licence
for this repository. Users should obtain an appropriate FFmpeg distribution
and comply with the applicable LGPL/GPL licence terms.

Other Python packages and libraries used by the project remain subject to
their respective licences.
